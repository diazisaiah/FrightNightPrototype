Voxel Blocks

The menus are made of cubes like the fighters and monsters. Buttons are chunky blocks with a lit top and a darker side, and they sink into the screen when pressed. Every panel and card has stepped, pixel-cut corners. Cards get a thick block band in their rarity colour. Goofy comes from motion: buttons hop on hover and squash on press, coins pop, cards wobble. Graveyard comes from bone-white blocks and little cube skulls.

Polished Camp

The look you have now, done properly. Anton on every button and tab, bigger tap targets, buttons with a soft bevel that squish when pressed and glow gold on hover. Cards get a rarity glow, a coloured tag for their type and a darker well for the stats. Pill-shaped coin counters, a bigger and brighter dialog title.
