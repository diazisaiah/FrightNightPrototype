# Playable Fighter Versus Portraits

These art-only portraits are used on the player side of the Dead League versus splash. They intentionally live separately from the class-select cards so each screen can be tuned independently.

## Shared direction

- 3:4 vertical composition, exactly one living human fighter, centered and framed knees-up.
- Dramatic low camera angle, both hands and the full head inside frame, strong readable silhouette.
- Polished high-detail small-cube voxel construction matching the Dead League boss portraits.
- Signature-color aura, cinematic rim light, near-black underground-arena background, soft black edge fade.
- Premium fighting-game key art with grounded boxing gear and a slight horror-comedy edge.
- No baked-in text, letters, numbers, logo, UI, card border, watermark, extra people, undead traits, crosses, or religious symbols.
- Correct anatomy and exactly five fingers per hand where visible.

## The Mauler — `fighter_berserker.png`

Massive heavyweight with a square shaved dark head, deep brown skin, ember-orange eyes, enormous battered red boxing gloves, red-and-white taped cuffs, bare powerful forearms, and a dark sleeveless fight tunic with waist sash. Hard guard, fiery orange-red aura, sparks and smoke.

## The Blur — `fighter_combo_master.png`

Lean young combination boxer with spiky black hair, glowing violet eyes, dark navy-violet sleeveless training gear, and white hand wraps. Compact rapid-combo stance with translucent violet fist afterimages and time streaks while the real hands remain clear.

## The Answer — `fighter_counter_master.png`

Composed counter fighter with medium-brown skin, short squared black hair, cyan eyes, black gloves taped white over the knuckles, cyan-blue forearm wraps, and a dark sleeveless gi-like coat. Tight disciplined guard with restrained blue geometric shards.

## The Stray — `fighter_free_runner.png`

Lean parkour boxer with spiky dark hair, green eyes, red bandana and two trailing tails, dark sleeveless hooded top with green trim, green fingerless gloves, and white forearm tape. Forward dash-punch with green wind ribbons and speed trails.

## Tony — `fighter_wise_guy.png`

Stocky middle-aged New Yorker with stubble, heavy brows, black knit cap, charcoal tracksuit with cream piping, white wraps, and one simple gold chain. One fist guards while the other hand makes a five-fingered palm-up “fuggedaboudit” gesture; amber-gold gym haze and a cocky smirk.
