# Dead League portrait prompts

One portrait per contender for the loading screen. Save each as a PNG over the placeholder of the same name in this folder:

| File | Contender |
|---|---|
| `portrait_0.png` | The Bellman |
| `portrait_1.png` | The Headless |
| `portrait_2.png` | Sister Southpaw |
| `portrait_3.png` | The Plague Doctor |
| `portrait_4.png` | The Coffin King |

The game picks them up with no code changes.

**Format:** portrait orientation (about 3:4, e.g. 1152×1536), the figure centered from the knees up. Use a transparent or near-black background with a soft dark edge so it fades into the loading screen.

**Shared style (put this first in every prompt):**

> Voxel / blocky pixel-art style undead boxer, dramatic horror-comedy fighting-game character portrait, built from small cubes like the Dead League key art, glowing rim light in its signature color, fists up in a boxing guard, embers and wisps of colored aura around it, low camera angle, dark moody background fading to black, high detail, no text, no crosses or religious symbols.

## The Bellman (red / orange)

> A huge hulking undead prizefighter made of grey stone-like blocks, glowing molten-orange ribs visible through a cracked chest, a heavy brass bell hanging on thick gold chains across his chest, red-and-white wrapped forearms and giant red boxing fists, tattered dark red robe at the waist, glowing red eyes in a blocky skull face, fiery red-orange aura and sparks.

## The Headless (blue)

> A lean undead knight-boxer with no head: a floating blue-flame skull hovers above his shoulders with glowing cyan eye sockets, ghostly blue flames pouring off the shoulders and arms, bone ribs over a dark navy tunic, a skirt of tattered blue and white strips, white-taped fists in a tight guard, cold blue spectral aura.

## Sister Southpaw (purple)

> An undead nun boxer in a torn violet and black habit with a plain white headband and long dark hair under the veil, glowing purple eyes, white-taped forearms, leading with her left fist in a southpaw stance, a simple gold chain around her neck with no pendant, swirling purple ghost-fire aura, the habit's hem shredded into wisps.

## The Plague Doctor (green)

> A hunched undead plague doctor boxer in a ragged dark hooded coat, a long bone-white bird-beak mask with one glowing green eye, a leather belt hung with small glowing green potion vials, bandaged forearms and dark fists raised, toxic green smoke and fumes curling around him, sickly green aura.

## The Coffin King (gold)

> A towering skeletal undead king-boxer with a jagged gold crown, a grinning skull with red eyes, heavy gold chains draped across a royal red and gold robe (no pendant), a gold skull belt buckle, huge gold-plated boxing gauntlets, a tattered red cape, golden-orange aura and drifting embers, regal and menacing.
