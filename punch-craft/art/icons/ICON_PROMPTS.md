# Upgrade & Skill Icons

Drop a PNG named `<icon>.png` in this folder and it replaces the vector icon of that name everywhere (upgrade web, skill webs, detail panel). Missing ones keep the old vector icon, so they can be done a few at a time.

## Shared direction (paste before each subject)

- A simple **flat UI icon**, like a menu or ability glyph. Not an illustration or a character.
- **One solid white shape** on a **transparent background**. No color, no gradients, no shading, no texture, no glow.
- Bold, chunky silhouette with thick even lines, slightly rounded corners and minimal inner detail: at most one or two cut-out lines.
- Front or side view, centered, filling about 75% of a 512×512 square, with a little breathing room.
- Must read instantly at 32 pixels.
- No text, numbers, borders, frames, background circles, people or faces (unless the subject is a face), crosses or religious symbols.

The game tints the white shape with the branch color (orange, cyan, violet...) and dims it when locked, so keep every icon pure white.

Example prompt: *"Simple flat UI icon, one solid white silhouette on a transparent background, bold chunky shape, minimal detail, no color or shading: a sledgehammer."*
