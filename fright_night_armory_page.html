ARMORY

Your gear has four slots (Gloves, Wraps, Boots, Charm). Wearing four pieces of one set turns on its bonus, and kept pieces can be levelled up with Souls or sold for coins. All three layouts use the Shop look (no big panel, translucent cards, turning 3D models on real cards). The coloured boxes stand in for those models.

A · PAPER DOLL

Your fighter in the middle with the four worn slots around them, like the attribute ring. Tap a slot and the right side shows only kept pieces for that slot. Set progress sits under the doll.
