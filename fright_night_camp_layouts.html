Three ways to lay out camp

The last rounds changed the paint and none of it landed, because the real problem is the layout. These three keep today's look exactly and only move things around. Each one is a full camp screen built on a real screenshot of your camp. Click the menus inside them; they work.

What's wrong now

Today every choice sits in one row: 7 tabs on the left and 7 buttons on the right, all the same size.

UpgradesDeckArmoryCosmeticsBountiesShopAttributes MenuLoadoutTrainingDailyDead LeagueArenaCrypt
  • The three ways to play (Arena, Crypt, Dead League) look like any other button, mixed in with Training and Menu.
  • Your fighter is split across four places: Attributes, Loadout, Armory, and changing fighter is behind Menu on the title screen.
  • Looking good is split in two: Cosmetics and Shop. Things to do are split in two: Bounties and Daily.
  • On a phone, 14 equal buttons in one row are tiny, and nothing tells you what to do next.
A

Home + Play

Camp opens on a home screen: your fighters at the fire in the middle, and the three ways to play as big picture cards on the right, with your progress on each. Everything about your fighter lives in a rail on the left. Settings is a gear, not a "Menu" that leaves camp.

Try it: click the left rail and the play cards.

B

Bottom Bar

The layout most phone games use. Five big buttons along the bottom with a raised gold PLAY in the middle. PLAY shows the three ways to play as tall cards, with Training and Daily under them. Goals is a bell in the top corner. Every page gets almost the whole screen.

Try it: click the bottom bar and the bell.

C

Camp Is the Menu

No menu bar at all. The camp scene itself is the menu: labels float over real places. The gate goes to the Arena, the Crypt door to the Crypt, a fight poster to the Dead League, the dummy to Training and Daily, the fire to your fighter and upgrades, the tent to your style and the shop, and a notice board to your goals. Pick one and the camera moves to that spot while its panel slides in.

Try it: click a label in the scene.

My pick: A, Home + Play

It fixes all four problems, keeps the campfire on screen, and works the same on desktop, controller and phone. C is the most special, so it could come later: once A exists, turning the play cards into places in the scene is a natural next step. B is the safest for phones but makes camp feel like a generic mobile game.
