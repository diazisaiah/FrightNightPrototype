FIGHT HUD ON A REAL FIGHT

Three HUD looks laid over an actual Gravedigger bout frame. Use the buttons to switch fighter (each has its own ultimate aura), fill the ultimate, run a passive cooldown, fire a power, spend Gale charges, or switch to the phone layout. The buttons change all three looks at once.

Dark leather pieces with a stitched dashed seam. This is the closest to what you asked for and fits the camp's warm wood and fire.

Cream boxing-wrap tape, slightly crooked, like hand wraps on fight night. It's the boldest and reads best over dark arenas, but it's the furthest from the camp UI.

Slim TV-broadcast plates with a color edge, slanted bars and a single stitch line. It's the cleanest and covers the least of the fight, and the stitching is only a hint.

SAME IN ALL THREE

The passive greys out on use and refills left to right. Timed powers grey out with a clock sweep, and Gale and Iron fill from the bottom by charges. Each fighter's aura plays on a full ultimate. On phones the sticks have no MOVE / LOOK text.
