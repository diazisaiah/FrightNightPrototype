Fright Night · camp pass 2 · the Fighter page

Three ways to draw your fighter

Each screen below is the new Fighter page in the camp, with the near-transparent look of the Upgrades web: the camp shows through, thin lines, glowing nodes, a detail panel on the right. Deck is now a switch at the top (ATTRIBUTES | DECK). The side rail is evenly spaced with the new icons, "Camp" is gone, and your fighter is the real voxel Mauler. Click around: tap attributes, press + to spend a point, switch to Deck, tap cards.

A

Ring

The upgrade web, but for your fighter. They stand in the middle, the five attributes are glowing nodes round them, and each has three small perk dots that light up at 10, 20 and 30 points. Tap a node and the right panel shows what it does, the perks and a + button. Tap your fighter to see their stats.

Try it: tap a node, press +, tap your fighter, switch to Deck.
Good atMatches the Upgrades page exactly, so the camp feels like one thing. Fastest to read: all five attributes visible at once, perks as dots.
Watch outPerk text is only in the side panel, so you tap before you can read it.
PhoneGreat: big round tap targets, and the ring fits the middle of the screen.
B

Character Sheet

Your fighter stands on the left with their stats under them. The five attributes are slim see-through rows on the right, each with a bar marked at 10, 20 and 30 and the three perk names right under it, lighting up as you earn them. Nothing to tap but +, and everything is readable without opening anything.

Try it: press + on a row, switch to Deck.
Good atThe most readable: every perk, number and bar is on screen at once. Easiest to scan for "what do I put points in".
Watch outIt reads like a list again (nicer than today, but still a list). The least like the Upgrades web.
PhoneRows are wide and short, so on a phone the perk names get small; the fighter would shrink or hide.
C

Around the Body

The fighter stands in the centre and the attributes are plaques floating either side with lines to the part of the body they belong to: Power to the fists, Grit to the chest, Speed to the boots, Technique to the eyes, Fortune to the belt pocket. Each plaque shows its number, three perk bars and the perk names.

Try it: tap a plaque to select it, press + to spend a point, switch to Deck.
Good atThe most memorable and the goofiest: the lines to the body make it feel like a character screen from an RPG.
Watch outTwo columns of wide plaques leave the fighter squeezed in the middle; the lines need to be drawn carefully for each fighter's body.
PhoneOK: the plaques shrink to number, icon and perk bars; names appear when selected.
My pick: A, the Ring

It copies the part you said you like, it is the same pattern as the Upgrades page (so the camp learns one way of working), and it works best on a phone. If readability matters more than matching, B shows everything without a tap. C could come later as a skin.
